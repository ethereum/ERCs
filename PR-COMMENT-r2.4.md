Revision after the first Magicians round (thread: https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/erc-8434-agent-identity-aid/29805):

- **Authority intervals** (per @chugarchugarr): a binding confers authority only while its predicate holds; re-establishing it opens a new interval and never authorizes the gap. `agentId`-keyed evidence observed outside every interval is not attributable to the AID. No new on-chain state — intervals are reconstructed from `Bound`/`Unbound` and ERC-8004 `Transfer` / `MetadataSet(agentWallet)` events (§3, §11, §12, Rationale, Security).
- **Stale-binding takeover**: `bind`/`bindWithSig` may take over a binding whose predicate has failed for its holder (emits `Unbound` for the old anchor); refused while the old anchor still owns or is the wallet of the agent. Behavioural change to the reference registry; interface and interfaceId unchanged (§3, §4).
- **Timing, orthogonal to provenance** (per @babyblueviper1): optional `subjectWindow` and `committedAt {anchor: block|rfc3161|ots, proof, log?}` on the facet envelope; resolvers report `pre-outcome` only when the commitment verifies earlier than `subjectWindow.until`, else `integrity-only`; existence ≠ exclusivity noted (§6, §8, §9, Security).
- Assets: `AIDRegistry.sol`, mock registry (now emits ERC-8004-style events), `facet.schema.json`, reference resolver (interval reconstruction, block-anchored commitment verification), vectors and two new fixtures. Test suite 22 → 28 cases.
